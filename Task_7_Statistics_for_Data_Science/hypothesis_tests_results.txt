Week 7 Hypothesis Test Results
Significance level: 0.05

Test Name: One-sample t-test: mean sale vs 100,000
H0: Mean Total_Sales = 100,000
H1: Mean Total_Sales != 100,000
Test Used: one-sample t-test
Test Statistic: 2.361244
P-value: 0.0201722
Decision: Reject H0
Business Interpretation: The average transaction differs statistically from the 100,000 benchmark.

Test Name: One-way ANOVA: sales across regions
H0: All regional mean sales are equal
H1: At least one regional mean differs
Test Used: one-way ANOVA
Test Statistic: 2.164363
P-value: 0.0972368
Decision: Fail to reject H0
Business Interpretation: Regional average sales show whether location is associated with different transaction values.

Test Name: Pearson correlation: Price and Total_Sales
H0: Population correlation is 0
H1: Population correlation is not 0
Test Used: Pearson correlation significance test
Test Statistic: 0.646131
P-value: 3.88216e-13
Decision: Reject H0
Business Interpretation: Price and Total_Sales have a statistically testable linear relationship.

Test Name: Chi-square: Contract and Churn
H0: Contract and Churn are independent
H1: Contract and Churn are associated
Test Used: chi-square test of independence
Test Statistic: 27.715301
P-value: 9.58735e-07
Decision: Reject H0
Business Interpretation: Contract type can be compared with churn patterns, without claiming causation.
